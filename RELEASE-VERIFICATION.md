# Quick Scan 1.2.72 finishing verification

Manually entered fillet radii and chamfer distances use 0.01 mm or 0.001 in increments. The editor blocks finer entries and reopens old drawings at their stored dimensions. The installed tests exercised both measurement systems, asymmetric chamfer reversal in the viewport and scan analysis on three saved projects. Fractional input is deferred.

The 512-part prismatic benchmark executed 25,600 requests. All 25,600 passed preview, commit, analytic volume and selected-edge clearance checks. The bench reopened 1,024 resulting STEP solids. Median preview time was 48 ms and p95 was 104 ms on the test machine. A separate native test compared 3,296 single-edge requests across 22 industrial and reconstructed parts against SOLIDWORKS 2026 SP4.1. Results matched volume, area and clearance tolerances on 2,805. In 239 further cases both tools built different geometry. Another 95 built only in SOLIDWORKS, 89 only in Quick Scan and 68 in neither.

Single-edge gear chamfers at 3.20 mm now continue across a narrow planar flank onto its angled neighbor. The gear benchmark improved from 103 to 106 matches in 108 requests. Eighteen rigid placements contributed 9,936 measured boundary points with a maximum difference of 0.00000213 mm. The installed test reopened three gear projects, edited their chamfers and committed the preview. The regression suite includes an analytic polygon and a rectangular negative control.

Variable fillets offer Smooth and Linear between stations. Linear is supported on a straight edge between planar supports with perpendicular ends. Eight increasing and decreasing control fillets matched independently measured SOLIDWORKS shapes. On the updated SOLIDWORKS 2027 SP0 session, 20 full-round, variable and disconnected controls matched Quick Scan. On a deliberately difficult selection of 49 requests, SOLIDWORKS built all 49, SolidCraft 0.49.1 built 36 and Quick Scan built 26. Of those 26 Quick Scan results, 25 met the comparison tolerances against SOLIDWORKS.

The general 99% feasible-model target remains open. Outstanding cases include torus-plane intersections, thin raised bosses and edges where a blend runs into another feature. General G2, hold-line and partial-edge methods remain outside the verified coverage. QUICKSURFACE comparisons covered the live workflow and analysis overlay. The reference programs and their private models are separate from this source distribution.

All 25 finishing regression phases passed on a complete repeat, including the mouse workflows and the installed desktop controls. The desktop and MCP Node suites passed 63 and 127 tests. A clean checkout rebuilt all 75 files in the installed web payload byte for byte. Its separately packaged desktop application passed the CAD-kernel startup and drawing save/reopen checks.
