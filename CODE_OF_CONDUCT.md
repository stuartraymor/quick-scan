# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
summarised here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Quick Scan to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep reports and discussion on topic. Share files you have the right to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate or harmful.

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers using the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (mark it as a conduct report) or by
contacting the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially and fairly.
