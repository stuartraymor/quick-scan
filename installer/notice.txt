Quick Scan - before you install

Quick Scan turns 3D scans into measured, editable CAD. It runs entirely on
your computer.

 * Early release. Quick Scan is version 0.0.x software. Keep copies of your
   source scans and save projects often.
 * Open source. Quick Scan is a modified version of ScanRuler by Stefan Hermann
   (CNC Kitchen), licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0.
   The next page shows the licence. The complete source code is available from
   the project page linked in the Start Menu folder.
 * Independent. Quick Scan is not affiliated with or endorsed by CNC Kitchen or
   by any commercial scan-to-CAD vendor.
 * Unsigned installer. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC",
   this installer has no code-signing certificate yet. Compare the SHA-256
   shown in the release notes with your download before running it.
 * Uninstalling removes the program but keeps your projects and settings
   (stored under %APPDATA%\QuickScan-v...). Delete those folders by hand to
   remove everything.
